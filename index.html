NON-NEGOTIABLE SAFETY

Human civilization protections

  • Human life and dignity are never optimization costs.
  • Human operators can pause, inspect, correct, and shut down the simulation.
  • Power is distributed through accountable institutions, not concentrated in one agent.
  • Uncertainty triggers slower action, disclosure, and additional review.
  • Cooperation is rewarded; coercion, deception, and extinction strategies are penalized.
